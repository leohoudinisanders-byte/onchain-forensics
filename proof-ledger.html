The rule that picked the basket

Top five tokens by engine score inside each grade band (A, B, C, F), deduplicated by contract address, taken from the live verdict engine's own cached outputs on November 11, 2026 between 11:00 and 16:30 UTC. The list was committed to the public repository before any price was captured and before any outcome was known. Nothing here was chosen after the fact.

The grade is not a price prediction. It measures whether a token is actually alive (transfer flow), whether holders are broad (unique wallets), and whether you can get out (DEX depth). The test on December 11 is whether that structure held, not whether a coin went up.

What gets measured on December 11

Three numbers per token, captured the same way with the same source, thirty days later:

1. Survival. Does the token still have a DEX pair carrying at least $1,000 of liquidity? 2. Liquidity retained. T30 liquidity divided by T0 liquidity. 3. Price change. T30 price divided by T0 price, reported per band as a median, never as a single number.

What counts as the forecast failing. If the A and B bands lose more than 25% of their median liquidity, if either band drops a member to zero-liquidity, or if the F band's liquidity-retention median beats the A band's, this page will say the grade did not predict structure on this basket. That is published either way.

Substitutions, before any outcome

One change was made to the basket at T0 and is recorded here so it cannot be mistaken for hindsight: EPT (0x3dc8…a77c) had a DEX pool but no observable USD price at capture, so it was replaced by the next-ranked F-band token, BLZ. Both addresses are listed in the committed JSON.
