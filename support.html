What counts as a bug on our side

  • A paid call returning an empty, null, or zero value where the chain was readable. We treat that as a defect, not a data point: unreadable reads are reported as partial or unavailable in the payload, never as a zero.
  • A 402 challenge that does not settle, or a settlement that is taken without an answer.
  • A response that does not match the published schema in openapi.json.
  • A grade that ignores a material fact you can point to on-chain. Send the address, the block, and the fact; we will re-measure and publish the correction.

What we cannot help with

We do not give investment advice, we do not tell you to buy or sell, and we do not recover funds. The service measures on-chain facts and publishes them; what you do with that is yours.
