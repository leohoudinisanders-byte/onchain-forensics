What grade F means here

An F is the bottom band. The token is essentially unreadable as a market: one or two transfers, or none, across the whole window. A handful of transfers, but too few wallets to call a market. Low activity scores catch tokens that look alive on a chart while almost nobody is actually moving them, which is the shape of both an abandoned project and a wash-traded one.

For mETH specifically, the ethereum median score is 20 across 302 tokens and the highest score on the chain is 100. mETH sits -3 against that median, and it is one of 62 ethereum tokens currently graded F.

How this was measured

The scanner reads transfer logs for the contract over the last 500 blocks and counts transfers, distinct sending and receiving wallets, mints, burns, and the share held by the largest wallet. Blocks are pulled in chunks against a private Alchemy endpoint with public gateways only as fallback, and coverage is recorded per call: if the reads do not cover the window, the response says PARTIAL instead of inventing a quiet token.

A 500-block window means different wall-clock time per chain. On Ethereum that is the recent past rather than a daily average, which is the point: activity is the one thing a price chart cannot show you and a token list cannot fake.
