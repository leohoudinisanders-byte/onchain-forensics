What grade D means here

A D means the activity signal is weak: either nothing moved or almost nothing did. Treat every D as a liquidity question first and a price question second. Nothing moved. Over the last 500 blocks there were no transfers to score, so the token grades D. That is not proof of fraud; it is proof of no market. A dormant token is hard to exit at any price because there is nobody on the other side of the trade.

For FARM specifically, the base median score is 5 across 198 tokens and the highest score on the chain is 94. FARM sits +0 against that median, and it is one of 158 base tokens currently graded D.

How this was measured

The scanner reads transfer logs for the contract over the last 500 blocks and counts transfers, distinct sending and receiving wallets, mints, burns, and the share held by the largest wallet. Blocks are pulled in chunks against a private Alchemy endpoint with public gateways only as fallback, and coverage is recorded per call: if the reads do not cover the window, the response says PARTIAL instead of inventing a quiet token.

A 500-block window means different wall-clock time per chain. On Base that is the recent past rather than a daily average, which is the point: activity is the one thing a price chart cannot show you and a token list cannot fake.
