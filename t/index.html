Token activity grades

500 tokens measured on Ethereum and Base, refreshed roughly every six hours. Sorted by activity score. Grade distribution A: 4 · B: 6 · C: 86 · D: 313 · F: 91.

Each grade answers one question a price chart cannot: is anything actually moving, and across how many wallets? A token can be up 40% on the day and still grade D because four wallets traded it. That is the point of measuring the tape instead of the ticker.
