1. What the service is

On-chain Forensics ("the service") answers questions about public blockchain state: token grades, supply integrity, counterparty settlement checks, wallet flows. Answers are computed from public chain data and returned over HTTPS, with payment handled per call through x402 in USDC on Base.

3. Data accuracy and no warranty

Reads are made against public chain state through third-party RPC providers. We work to make them accurate and to mark unreadable data as partial or unavailable rather than zero, but the service is provided "as is" and "as available", without warranties of any kind, express or implied, including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, and non-infringement.

A grade is a measurement at a point in time, not a guarantee of future behaviour. Tokens can change contract behaviour, ownership, and supply after a read. A cached grade is labelled as cached and carries the block it was measured at.
