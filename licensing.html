What it is not

These are activity measurements, not price predictions and not investment advice. We will not license a claim that a token is "safe", only what we can measure: whether it moved, how many wallets moved it, who is minting, and whether the reads were complete. A token can grade A and still go to zero on news; that is a different question from liquidity, and we are honest about which one we answer.

Why not just use the public endpoint

You can. Every route takes an x402 payment in USDC for $0.001 to $0.25, and a free developer key covers 10,000 cached calls a month. The license exists for the case where the payment prompt is the problem: your users should never see a 402, and you should not be signing a payment per screen refresh.
